Privacy notice

Oranje People · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This notice explains what personal data the Oranje People app (and its web version at mi.oranjepeople.com) collects, what we use it for and who we share it with. The app is used by the workers who work with us at hotels and by the hotel staff who supervise them.

What we collect

  • Account: your email and password to sign in.
  • Profile: name, phone, address, profile photo, means of transport and the details your recruiter records (birth date, English level, experience).
  • Tax document: the image of your SSN or ITIN, to meet tax and payroll obligations.
  • Health and emergencies: your blood type, optional medical notes and the name, phone and relationship of your emergency contact.
  • Punches: your clock-in, lunch and clock-out times, your precise location at the moment you punch and, depending on the hotel, a selfie or the hotel's QR code.
  • Work at the hotel: schedules, timesheets, reviews, requisitions and notes recorded by hotel staff.

Location, camera and notifications

  • Location is taken only when you punch, to confirm you are at the hotel. The app never records it in the background.
  • The camera is used for the punch selfie, to read the hotel's QR code and to photograph your document or profile photo. When you take the selfie, the app only checks on your phone that your face is centered; no biometric data is stored. What is sent is the photo.
  • Notifications let you know what happens with your work, your requisitions or your staff.

What we use it for

  • Recording your attendance and calculating your hours.
  • Assigning you to hotels and coordinating your work with them.
  • Paying your wages and meeting tax and labor obligations.
  • Helping you in an emergency.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not use third-party tracking or analytics tools.

Who we share it with

  • The hotel where you work: it sees your name, photo, position, schedules and punches.
  • Our payroll provider, to pay you and make the required withholdings.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase, which host our servers, files and sign-in.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How we protect it and how long we keep it

Data travels encrypted and files (photos and documents) are kept in private storage; only authorized staff can see them. We keep it while you work with us and afterwards for as long as tax and labor laws require.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to close your account. Write to us at privacidad@oranjepeople.com. Some data, such as payroll records, must be kept even if you close your account, for the period the law requires.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date above and let you know in the app when the change is significant.
